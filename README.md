# Tiger & Goat Summit

Prompt: A high-quality 3D isometric screenshot of a digital board game in a vibrant, low-poly art style. The setting is a bright, sunny outdoor environment.

The Board: In the center is a thick, square wooden game board. Carved into the wood is a geometric grid consisting of a large triangle on top of a rectangular grid, with visible circular nodes at the intersections. A faint etching of a tiger is visible on the top left of the wooden board.

The Pieces: There are low-poly 3D animal figures standing on circular bases at the node intersections. At the top of the board (inside the triangle section) are 3 orange Tigers. Spread across the lower horizontal lines of the grid are 8 white Goats.

The User Interface (UI):

Top Left: A dark grey square button with a white gear icon.

Top Center: A dark grey pill-shaped banner reading "TURN 01" in white and yellow text, with a small icon of two people.

Top Right: Two dark grey square buttons; one with an undo arrow, one with a question mark.

Middle Left: A dark grey panel with a reddish-brown header reading "TIGERS" next to a tiger head icon. Below it, white text says "3 Tigers", followed by a line, and "Objective: Capture 5 Goats".

Middle Right: A dark grey panel with a blue header reading "GOATS" next to a goat head icon. Below it, white text says "15 Goats", followed by a line, and "Objective: Survive or Trap Tigers".

Bottom Center: A large, bright green button reading "YOUR TURN".

Bottom Row Buttons: Light grey pill-shaped buttons flanking the center. On the left: "RULES" (book icon) and "HISTORY" (clock icon). On the right: "RESET" (loop arrow) and "NEW GAME" (crossed swords).

Background: A beautiful, slightly blurred low-poly landscape with green trees, stone pathways, and traditional Asian-style temples/tents with orange roofs. Large red banners with gold emblems flank the bottom left and right corners of the screen. The lighting is crisp, casting sharp shadows from the game pieces onto the wooden board.
